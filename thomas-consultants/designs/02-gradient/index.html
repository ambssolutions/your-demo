Recent work in detail

Engineering that gets signed off.

At Thom Street in New Lynn we designed the earthworks, water, wastewater, stormwater and roading for 82 terrace houses and apartments for Kāinga Ora, monitored construction, and obtained sign-offs from Watercare, Auckland Council and Auckland Transport. The site opened in November 2020.

Read the case study

Ecology that protects what is already there.

Kauri Glen Reserve in Northcote has one of the highest concentrations of kauri in New Zealand, some 300 to 400 years old. Our ecological assessment for Auckland Council covered 5,905 m of track repairs, 490 m of new track and an 18 metre high bridge, with the spread of kauri dieback in mind.

Read the case study

Public spaces people come back to.

For Auckland Council and the Ōrakei Local Board, we took the Churchill Park lookout in Glendowie through detailed design and construction. It looks out over a former golf course to Rangitoto, Karaka Bay and the city, and gives visitors seating, shade and a little history.
