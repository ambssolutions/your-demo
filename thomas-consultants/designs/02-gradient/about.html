Thomas Consultants is a land development consultancy with offices in Auckland, Manukau and Christchurch. We help clients turn land into homes, parks and places that work.

Our story

We start every project on the ground.

For 25 years we have worked closely with our clients to understand their vision and help them create smarter, more sustainable spaces.

Our engineers, planners, surveyors, landscape architects, ecologists and contamination experts support you through the whole journey, from site assessment to final delivery.

Community & sustainability

Good development leaves the land better.

We are committed to environmentally responsible and sustainable development. Our ecologists and contamination specialists find environmental risks early, and our landscape architects design open spaces that communities use and enjoy.

Our Toitū Envirocare accreditation holds us to account on our own environmental footprint, and ISO 45001 keeps our people and sites safe.
