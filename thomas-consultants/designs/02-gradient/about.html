A land development consultancy with every discipline under one roof.

Thomas Consultants has worked on land development and infrastructure projects for 25 years, from a head office in Auckland, an office in Manukau and a satellite office in Christchurch.

Why the disciplines sit in one office

A typical subdivision or housing project needs a surveyor, a planner and a civil engineer. Many also need a landscape architect, an ecologist or someone to test the soil. When those people work for different firms, the client spends a lot of time passing drawings between them.

Our engineers, planners, surveyors, landscape architects, ecologists and contamination specialists work together. At Scott Road in Hobsonville that meant planning, surveying, engineering, landscape architecture, traffic engineering and soil testing for 67 dwellings came from one team, and the resource consent was approved on 9 February 2022.

On 10 new homes for Signature Homes and Kāinga Ora in Point Chevalier, the same team did the planning, the engineering design and the construction monitoring. Senior Design Engineer Sam Liu and Lead Construction Monitoring Engineer Advait Dolas saw it through to the certifications from Council, Watercare and Auckland Transport.
