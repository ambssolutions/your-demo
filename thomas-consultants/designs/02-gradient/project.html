Scott Road, Hobsonville.

Resource consent for a 67-home development, approved on 9 February 2022.

Outcome
Resource consent approved on 9 February 2022.

Thomas Consultants provided its full range of land development services to obtain resource consent for a 67-home development in Hobsonville.

The scope included earthworks over 14,096 m² for future homes, road platforms and several retaining walls, with groundwater diversion and dewatering. The project was large and complex, and drew on our planning, surveying, engineering, landscape architecture, traffic engineering and soil testing teams, working with OZAC Architects.

The resource consent was approved on 9 February 2022.
