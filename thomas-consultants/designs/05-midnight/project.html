Case study · New Lynn

Thom Street, New Lynn.

Engineering design and construction monitoring for 82 terrace houses and apartments for Kāinga Ora.

Site
Thom Street, New Lynn, Auckland
Client
Kāinga Ora
Our role
Engineering design (drainage and public road), construction monitoring
Completed
November 2020
The project

What we did.

Thomas Consultants undertook the engineering design and construction monitoring for the public infrastructure serving 82 terrace houses and apartments for Kāinga Ora. The development was built to house 300 Kāinga Ora tenants on an 11,046 m² site, and opened in November 2020.

The project includes 7 stand-alone two-storey houses, three two-level apartment blocks with 9 units each and three three-level blocks with 4 to 6 units each, all reached from a new public road. Our team was involved from inception to completion, covering earthworks, water, wastewater, stormwater and roading.

We prepared scheduled quantities and tender documents, monitored the works and obtained sign-offs from Watercare, Auckland Council and Auckland Transport, so titles could be released as soon as construction finished.

Site
10-22 Taurus Crescent, Beach Haven, Auckland
Client

Our role
Engineering, surveying and planning

The project

What we did.

Case study · Glendowie

Churchill Park Lookout, Glendowie.

Detailed design and construction delivery of a lookout with 360° views over a former golf course.

Site
320-336 Riddell Road, Glendowie, Auckland
Client
Auckland Council, Ōrakei Local Board
Our role
Detailed landscape design and construction delivery
Partners
Urban Effects (seating)
The project

What we did.

Auckland Council asked us to deliver a lookout at Churchill Park, a large park in Glendowie with a rich history. Once a golf course, the park is now used as an urban farm. A golf clubhouse once sat on its highest point.

The lookout shows off 360° views to Karaka Bay, Rangitoto Island, Motukorea (Browns Island), Musick Point, Maungarei (Mount Wellington) and the Auckland CBD, with seating, shade and a place to rest.

We picked the project up at developed design stage, after it had been workshopped with the Ōrakei Local Board, and consulted with mana whenua as partners throughout.

Case study · Northcote

Kauri Glen Reserve, Northcote.

An ecological impact assessment for track renewals in a forest with kauri aged 300 to 400 years.

Site
Kauri Glen Reserve, Northcote, Auckland
Client
Auckland Council
Our role
Ecological impact assessment
The project

What we did.

Auckland Council commissioned us to assess the ecological effects of renewing a popular walking track. Because of kauri dieback, new tracks and an 18 metre high bridge were planned so visitors could enjoy the reserve without endangering native plants.

The renewals cover 5,905 m of existing track, 490 m of new realigned track and the decommissioning of 526 m. The reserve is a Significant Ecological Area with some of the highest concentrations of kauri in New Zealand, and the Onepoto Stream runs through it.

Our assessment found that, provided its recommendations and the arborist's advice are followed, the effects would be small and temporary.
