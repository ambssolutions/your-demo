Process

From bare site to delivered place.

  1. Stage 01

    Understand the site

    Topographical survey, drone inspection, contamination screening and a feasibility read on what the land can become.

  2. Stage 02

    Design & consent

    Planning strategy, engineering and landscape design, and resource consent applications handled by people who know council.

  3. Stage 03

    Deliver on site

    Set out, earthworks, roads and three waters, with construction monitoring by the engineers who designed it.

  4. Stage 04

    Complete & hand over

    Sign-off, as-builts and handover. We manage the project through to completion so nothing is left loose.
