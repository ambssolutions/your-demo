Health and safety

Safe sites, every visit.

Our surveyors, engineers and ecologists spend much of the week on live construction sites, in streams and beside busy roads. Our health and safety system is certified to ISO 45001, and we are members of SiteSafe and SiteWise.

Construction monitoring is part of the same discipline. It manages risk while the works are built, so problems are found early instead of fixed after the fact.

When the works are done, the same engineers who designed them collect the certificates council needs. Nothing is handed over until it is signed off.

Council approvals

The approvals we handle for you.

Four stages, and the consents and certificates that come with each.

01

Understand the site

Before any design, we find out what the land is and what the rules allow.

  • Topographical survey
  • Site investigation and soil testing
  • Feasibility and zoning advice
02

Design & consent

We design the scheme and secure the approvals council needs before work starts.

  • Resource consent
  • Engineering plan approval
  • Applications to Auckland Council, Watercare and Auckland Transport
03

Deliver on site

The engineers who designed the works watch them being built.

  • Set out
  • Construction observation and monitoring
  • As-built surveys
04

Complete & hand over

We collect the certificates that let titles be issued.

  • Completion certificates
  • s223 and 224c certificates
  • Release of titles
