About us

25 years of helping land become better places.

Engineers, planners, surveyors, landscape architects, ecologists and contamination experts, working together in one team.

Our story

A quarter of a century on the ground.

For 25 years we have delivered multi-faceted land development and infrastructure projects: parks, housing, commercial sites and public infrastructure.

We work closely with clients to understand their vision. Then we help them create smarter, more sustainable spaces, from site assessment to final delivery.

Our head office is in Auckland, with an office in Manukau and a satellite office in Christchurch. We work for councils, agencies, developers and private landowners.

Community & sustainability

Better spaces for the people who use them.

Many of our projects are parks, reserves and homes for New Zealand families. We take care with ecology, soil and water, and plan for the communities who live with the result long after we hand over.

Our environmental team tests and manages contaminated land so it can be used safely again. Our landscape architects design planting and open spaces that suit their place.
