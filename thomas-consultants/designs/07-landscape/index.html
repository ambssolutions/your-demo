Kauri Glen Reserve

5,905 mof existing track repaired and improved

Auckland Council asked us to assess the ecological effects of renewing the tracks through Kauri Glen, one of the densest stands of kauri in New Zealand, with trees 300 to 400 years old. New tracks and an 18 metre high bridge keep walkers off the roots and help stop the spread of kauri dieback.

The reserve is a Significant Ecological Area, with the Onepoto Stream and its wetlands running through it. With our recommendations and the arborist advice followed, the renewals would have a small and temporary ecological effect.

Withers Reserve Footbridge

May 2021new footbridge certified, on time and on budget

A severe flood damaged the footbridge at Withers Reserve and closed the walkway to Kashmir Street. Auckland Council needed it back quickly.

Our engineers, surveyors and ecologists designed a replacement robust enough for fast consent, planned sediment and erosion control, and monitored construction through to final certification.

Rural subdivision, Oratia

4lifestyle blocks, each with on-site stormwater and wastewater

A large rural site with one house became four lifestyle blocks. Each has its own on-site stormwater and wastewater disposal, water supply and utilities.

Alongside the engineering design we prepared the overland flow path assessment, the stormwater mitigation report and a restoration management and planting plan for the stream.
