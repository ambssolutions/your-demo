Surveyors, planners and engineers working on Auckland sections for 25 years.

We are a land development consultancy with offices in Auckland and Manukau and a satellite office in Christchurch. One team covers the surveys, consents, engineering and environmental reports a subdivision needs.

The people on your job

Landscape architects and project managers work alongside them on parks, schools and larger sites.

Engineers

Design the drains, roads and earthworks, and monitor construction until council signs it off.

Surveyors

Topographical and boundary surveys, set out, drone inspections and the survey plans for new titles.

Planners

Advice on zoning and land use, resource consent applications and hearings evidence.

Environmental scientists

Site history reviews, soil and water testing, asbestos surveys and ecology reports.

Track record

As well as homeowners and small developers, we work for Kāinga Ora, Auckland Transport, Watercare, Panuku, NZ Transport Agency, the Ministry of Education, Waikato District Council, Rotorua Lakes Council, Hobsonville Point and Homes of Choice. We aim to deliver on time and on budget, and to keep you told what is happening.
