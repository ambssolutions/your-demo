Our story

Built over 25 years

Thomas Consultants has delivered land development and infrastructure projects for 25 years. We are a multi-disciplinary team of engineers, planners, surveyors, landscape architects, ecologists and contamination experts.

We work closely with our clients to understand their vision, then help them create smarter, more sustainable spaces. Councils, government agencies, developers and private landowners trust us to guide them from site assessment to final delivery.
