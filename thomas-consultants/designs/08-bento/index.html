How it works

Four simple stages.

Understand the site, design and consent, deliver on site, then complete and hand over. One team the whole way.

1Stage 01

Understand the site

We walk the land, survey it and test it, so you know what it can do before you commit.

  • Survey and site investigation
  • Feasibility and constraints
  • Goals, budget and programme agreed
2Stage 02

Design & consent

We design the engineering and landscape, then prepare and lodge your resource consent.

  • Planning, engineering and landscape prepared together
  • Resource consent lodged with council
  • Council questions answered for you
3Stage 03

Deliver on site

We monitor construction and manage the programme so the work matches the approved plans.

  • Construction monitoring
  • Contractors and inspections coordinated
  • Programme kept on track
4Stage 04

Complete & hand over

We sign off the works, help with titles and hand over a finished, compliant project.

  • Final sign-offs
  • Survey for title
  • A finished, compliant project handed over
