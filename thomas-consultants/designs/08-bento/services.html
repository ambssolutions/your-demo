FAQ

Questions we hear often.

Plain answers to common questions. Ask us anything else in a free consultation.

Do I need a resource consent?

It depends on your zoning, what you plan to build and the rules in your district plan. Many subdivisions, new homes and earthworks need one. Our planners can check your site and tell you early, often in a complimentary planner meeting.

How long does a subdivision take?

Every site is different. Timing depends on the size of the subdivision, council processing, the engineering works and title issue. We give you a realistic programme at the start and keep you updated as it moves.

What is a cross-lease to freehold conversion?

On a cross-lease title you share ownership of the land with your neighbours. Converting to freehold gives each owner their own title. Our surveyors prepare the plans and we manage the process through to new titles.

Do you work outside Auckland?

Yes. We have offices in Auckland and Manukau, a satellite office in Christchurch, and we work with councils across New Zealand, including Waikato, Rotorua and New Plymouth.

What happens in the free development consultation?

We talk through your site, your idea, your timing and your budget. You leave with a clear view of the next steps and the specialists you will need.
