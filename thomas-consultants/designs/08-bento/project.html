Scott Road, Hobsonville.

Resource consent for a 67-dwelling development, with earthworks over 14,096 m².

Planning something similar?
See all our projects

Thomas Consultants provided its full range of land development services for a 67-dwelling development in Hobsonville, with the aim of obtaining resource consent.

The scope included earthworks over 14,096 m² to prepare the site for homes and roads, several retaining walls and the groundwater diversion that comes with them.

Planning, surveying, engineering, landscape architecture, traffic engineering and soil testing all came from our team. The consent was approved on 9 February 2022.

What we did

  • Planning and resource consent
  • Earthworks and retaining design
  • Traffic engineering
  • Soil testing
  • Landscape architecture
