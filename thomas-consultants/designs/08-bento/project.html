Thom Street, New Lynn

Public infrastructure for 82 terrace houses and apartments, homes for about 300 Kāinga Ora tenants.

About the project

Thomas Consultants undertook the engineering design and construction monitoring for the public infrastructure serving 82 terrace houses and apartments for Kāinga Ora, on an 11,046 m² site in New Lynn. The homes were built for about 300 Kāinga Ora tenants and are reached by a new public road.

Our team was involved from inception to completion: earthworks, water, wastewater, stormwater and roading. We used three-dimensional modelling to rationalise the design, prepared scheduled quantities and tender documents, and monitored construction.

We obtained sign-offs from Watercare, Auckland Council and Auckland Transport, and worked closely with the client and contractors so the project finished on time and on budget, with titles released as quickly as possible.
