Process

Four moves. One clear path.

A single team carries your project from first look to finished ground, so every handover is internal and every decision is informed.

  1. STEP 01

    Site assessment

    Survey, contamination testing, ecology and feasibility. We read the land before anyone draws on it.

  2. STEP 02

    Consent

    Zoning advice, resource consent applications and hearings evidence, managed by our RMA planners.

  3. STEP 03

    Design

    Roads, three waters, earthworks and landscape design, engineered to be robust and compliant.

  4. STEP 04

    Delivery

    Construction monitoring, set out and project management through to completion and sign-off.
