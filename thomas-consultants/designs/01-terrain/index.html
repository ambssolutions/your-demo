One project, start to finish

Thom Street, New Lynn

Thom Street, New Lynn. 82 terrace houses and apartments for Kāinga Ora, homes for about 300 tenants. This is how they came together.

82homes for Kāinga Ora
11,046 m²site in New Lynn
Nov 2020completed and opened
01

The brief

Kāinga Ora needed homes for about 300 tenants on an 11,046 m² site in New Lynn: 7 stand-alone two-storey houses, three two-level apartment blocks of 9 units and three three-level blocks of 4 to 6 units, all reached by a new public road. Our team was involved from inception to completion.

Engineers and planners reviewing plans around a table

02

Design

We designed the public infrastructure for all 82 homes: earthworks, water, wastewater, stormwater and roading. Software and three-dimensional modelling let the team test options quickly and settle on an efficient, rational design.

Hands pointing at a landscape plan on a desk

03

Consents and approvals

The project had to meet the requirements of its resource, engineering and building consents. We managed compliance throughout, with an eye on the finish line: titles released as soon as construction was complete.

Project team meeting in a boardroom

04

Construction

We prepared the scheduled quantities and tender documents, then monitored construction of the works, working closely with Kāinga Ora and the contractors to keep the project on time and on budget.

Road and kerb construction in progress

05

Handover

We obtained sign-offs from Watercare, Auckland Council and Auckland Transport. The site was completed and opened in November 2020.
