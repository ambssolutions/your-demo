Questions

Plain answers to common questions.

Do I need a resource consent?

It depends on your site's zoning and what you plan to do. Many subdivisions, new dwellings and earthworks need consent under the Resource Management Act. Our planners can review your site and tell you exactly what applies, starting with a complimentary planner meeting.

How long does a subdivision take?

Every site is different. Timeframes depend on the size of the subdivision, the consents required, infrastructure needs and council processing. We map out a realistic programme for your project at your free development consultation and keep you updated at every stage.

What is a cross-lease to freehold conversion?

Many older properties are held on cross-lease titles, where owners share the land and lease their buildings from each other. Converting to freehold gives each owner their own separate title. Our surveyors handle the survey and boundary work, and we coordinate the process with you.

Do you work outside Auckland?

Yes. Alongside our Auckland and Manukau offices we have a satellite office in Christchurch, and we work with councils and clients across New Zealand, including Waikato, Rotorua and New Plymouth.
