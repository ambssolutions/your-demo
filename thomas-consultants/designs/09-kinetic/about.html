Thomas Consultants is a land development consultancy with offices in Auckland, Manukau and Christchurch. This is who we are and how we work.

Our story

Land development
is what we do.

After 25 years of delivering multi-faceted land development and infrastructure projects, we know this work inside out.

We work closely with our clients to understand their vision and help them create smarter, more sustainable spaces. Engineers, planners, surveyors, landscape architects, ecologists and contamination experts support you through the entire journey, from site assessments to final delivery.
