Scott Road,Hobsonville.

Resource consent for a 67-dwelling development, with earthworks over 14,096 m². Approved 9 February 2022.

What we did

Planning, surveying, engineering, landscape architecture, traffic engineering and soil testing.

Thomas Consultants provided its full land development offering for a 67-dwelling development in Hobsonville, with the aim of obtaining resource consent.

The scope included earthworks over an area of 14,096 m² to prepare the site for the dwellings, roads and several retaining walls, with groundwater diversion and dewatering as part of the works.

The project was large and complex and needed a wide range of specialists. Our planners, surveyors, engineers, landscape architects, traffic engineers and soil testing team worked with OZAC Architects, and the consent was approved on 9 February 2022.
