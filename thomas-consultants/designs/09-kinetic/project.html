Consent, trafficand soil testingat Scott Road.

A development site in Hobsonville that needed planning, traffic engineering and environmental input to move forward.

(02.07) Case study

Planning
Resource Consent
Engineering
Traffic Engineering
Environmental

The brief

The site needed a resource consent, and council needed to understand traffic and ground conditions before it could decide.

Three specialist inputs had to line up: the planning case, the traffic assessment and the soil investigation. Any gap between them would cost time.

Our approach

  1. 01

    Resource consent

    Our RMA planners framed the proposal against the district plan and prepared the application.

  2. 02

    Traffic engineering

    Our engineers assessed access and traffic effects, and fed the findings straight into the consent.

  3. 03

    Soil testing

    Our environmental team tested the soil so ground conditions were understood early, not discovered late.

The outcome

One coordinated package, backed by evidence from our own planners, engineers and environmental scientists. One team to talk to, and no hand-offs between consultants.
