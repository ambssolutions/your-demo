Land Surveying

Accurate and reliable data for your development projects.

Good decisions start with good data. Our surveyors map your land, confirm its legal boundaries and set out the work on site, so designers and builders work from the same accurate picture.

  • Boundary redefinition and cross-lease to freehold
  • Topographical surveys and set out
  • Drone inspections and 3D laser scanning

Includes: Boundary redefinition, Cross-lease to freehold, Topographical surveys, Set out, Drone inspections, 3D laser scanning.

How we work

Four stages, from site to handover.

A closed traverse: one team carries your project through every stage.

01

Understand the site

We survey, test and investigate your land to understand its constraints and opportunities before you commit.

  • Topographical and boundary survey
  • Contamination and ecology checks
  • Feasibility advice
02

Design & consent

Our engineers, planners and landscape architects design the scheme together and prepare your resource consent application.

  • Engineering and landscape design
  • Resource consent application
  • Council liaison and hearings
03

Deliver on site

We set out the works, monitor construction and manage the programme so the build matches the design.

  • Set out
  • Construction monitoring
  • Programme and budget control
04

Complete & hand over

We close out the project with as-built information, council sign-off and titles, then hand everything over to you.

  • As-built surveys
  • Council sign-off
  • Titles and handover
