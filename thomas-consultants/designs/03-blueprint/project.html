Scott Road, Hobsonville.

Resource consent for 67 dwellings, with earthworks over 14,096 m². Approved 9 February 2022.

Case study

We provided our full range of services for a 67-dwelling development in Hobsonville, with the aim of obtaining resource consent.

The scope included earthworks over 14,096 m² for future homes, roads and several retaining walls, with groundwater diversion and dewatering.

Planning, surveying, engineering, landscape architecture, traffic engineering and soil testing came together in one team, and the consent was approved on 9 February 2022.
