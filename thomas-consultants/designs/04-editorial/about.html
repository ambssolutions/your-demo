Field Notes · About us

Twenty-five years of shaping land with care.

A land development consultancy with engineers, planners, surveyors, landscape architects, ecologists and contamination experts under one roof.

Our story

Built over 25 years, one project at a time.

After 25 years of delivering multi-faceted land development and infrastructure projects, our expertise is second to none.

We work closely with our clients to understand their vision and help them create smarter, more sustainable spaces. Our people support you through the entire journey, from site assessments to final delivery.

Today we work from our head office in Auckland, an office in Manukau and a satellite office in Christchurch, for councils, public agencies, developers and families.
