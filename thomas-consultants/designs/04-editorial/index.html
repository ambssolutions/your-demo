A place to rest, look out and remember.

Auckland Council asked us to deliver the design for a lookout at Churchill Park, a large park in Glendowie with a rich history. Once a golf course, the park is now an urban farm. A club house used to stand on its highest point.

The brief was a space that shows off the 360° views and tells visitors about the site's history, with seating, shade and a place to rest. The view takes in Karaka Bay, Rangitoto Island, Motukorea (Browns Island), Musick Point, Tahuna Torea Nature Reserve, Maungarei (Mount Wellington) and the city.

What you can see from the lookout

  • Karaka Bay
  • Rangitoto Island
  • Motukorea (Browns Island)
  • Musick Point
  • Tahuna Torea Nature Reserve
  • Maungarei (Mount Wellington)
  • Auckland's city centre

We picked the project up at developed design, after it had been workshopped with the Ōrakei Local Board, and consulted with mana whenua as partners through each stage. Our landscape architects carried it through detailed design and construction delivery.

Locals and visitors now use the lookout as a place to reflect and enjoy the views.

Ecology · Auckland Council

Kauri Glen: trees 300 to 400 years old.

An ecological assessment for track renewals in a Significant Ecological Area.

To protect the kauri from dieback, the council rebuilt the walking tracks and an 18 m high bridge. We assessed the impact of 5,905 m of repaired track and 490 m of new track, and found it would be small and temporary.
