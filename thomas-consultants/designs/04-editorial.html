As experienced land development specialists, we work closely with our clients to understand their vision and help them create smarter, more sustainable spaces.

Our team of engineers, planners, surveyors, landscape architects, ecologists, and contamination experts ensure you are supported throughout the entire journey, from site assessments to final delivery.

From head office in Auckland, our Manukau office and a satellite office in Christchurch, we bring quality civil engineering, resource consents and planning to projects of every scale, for councils, agencies, developers and families alike.

After 25 years of delivering multi-faceted land development and infrastructure projects, our expertise is second to none.

We are committed to environmentally responsible and sustainable development.

iii. The journey

Four chapters, one story.

01
Chapter one

Site assessment

We begin on the ground: survey, ecology and soil testing reveal what your land can carry, and where its opportunities and constraints lie.

Chapter two

Consent

Our planners shape a clear, well-evidenced resource consent application and guide it through council, including hearings if needed.

Chapter three

Design

Engineers and landscape architects resolve roads, three waters, earthworks and planting into one coordinated, buildable design.

Chapter four

Delivery

We set out, monitor construction and manage the programme through to completion, keeping you informed at every turn.
