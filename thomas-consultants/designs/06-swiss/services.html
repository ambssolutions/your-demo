05 Questions

Common questions, answered.

It depends on your site, its zoning and what you want to do. We check your plans against the Auckland Unitary Plan or the relevant district plan. Then we tell you if consent is needed, what type, and which reports council will expect. We can often answer this in a free development consultation.

Every site is different. A simple two-lot subdivision usually takes several months from survey to new titles. Larger or more complex sites take longer, mainly because of consent processing and construction. We give you a realistic programme at the start and manage it through to completion.

On a cross-lease, owners share the whole piece of land and lease their own dwelling. Converting to freehold gives each owner their own title. We survey the site, prepare the plans, manage any consent needed and lodge everything with Land Information New Zealand.

Yes. Our head office is in Auckland, we have an office in Manukau and a satellite office in Christchurch. We have worked with councils and agencies across the country, including Waikato District Council, Rotorua Lakes Council and New Plymouth District Council.

We talk through your site and what you want to achieve. We outline the options, likely consents, the specialists you will need and the next steps. There is no obligation to go further.
