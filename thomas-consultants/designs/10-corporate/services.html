Questions

Do I need a resource consent?

It depends on the zoning of the site and what you plan to do. Many subdivisions, earthworks and new dwellings need one. Our planners check the site against the district plan and tell you early.

How long does a subdivision take?

It depends on the number of lots, the consent, the infrastructure needed and council processing. We set out a programme at the start and update it as each approval comes through.

What is a cross-lease to freehold conversion?

On a cross-lease title owners share the land and lease their homes from the group. Converting to freehold gives each owner a separate title. We handle the survey and the subdivision consent.

Do you work outside Auckland?

Yes. Our Christchurch satellite office supports projects in Canterbury, and we have worked with Waikato District Council, Rotorua Lakes Council and New Plymouth District Council.

Can you act as Engineer to the Contract?

Yes. We have acted as Engineer to the Contract and Engineer’s Representative for Auckland Council, for example on the Evanda Link Reserve lighting renewal in Takanini.
