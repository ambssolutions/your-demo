FAQ

Questions we hear often.

Something else on your mind? Ask us at your free development consultation.

Contact our team

Do I need a resource consent?

It depends on your site's zoning and what you plan to do. Many subdivisions, earthworks and new dwellings need one. Our RMA planners check your site against the district plan and tell you early, so there are no surprises.

How long does a subdivision take?

Every site is different. Timing depends on the size of the subdivision, how complex the consent is, the infrastructure needed and council processing. The full journey from feasibility to new titles usually runs for many months. We give you a realistic programme at your free consultation.

What is a cross-lease to freehold conversion?

On a cross-lease title you share ownership of the land with your neighbours and lease your home from the group. Converting to freehold gives each owner a separate title. We handle the survey and the subdivision consent, and work with your lawyer on the paperwork.

Do you work outside Auckland?

Yes. Our Auckland Head Office and Manukau office cover the wider Auckland region, and our Christchurch satellite office supports projects in Canterbury. We also work with councils such as Waikato District Council and Rotorua Lakes Council.

What happens at the free development consultation?

We talk through your site, your idea, your timing and your budget. You leave with a clear view of the next steps and who on our team can help.
