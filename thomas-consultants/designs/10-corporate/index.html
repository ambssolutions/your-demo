Our process

A clear path from site assessment to delivery.

Every project follows a proven four-stage framework, with the same team accountable at every step.

How we work
  1. 01

    Understand the site

    We survey, test and investigate boundaries, topography, soils, contamination and ecology. You know what your land can do before you commit.

    Topographical survey · Feasibility report · Soil testing
  2. 02

    Design & consent

    Our planners, engineers and landscape architects shape the scheme and prepare the resource consent application, through to hearings evidence where needed.

    Resource consent · Three waters · Planting plans
  3. 03

    Deliver on site

    We set out the works, monitor construction and keep contractors, council and you on the same page.

    Set out · Construction monitoring · Earthworks
  4. 04

    Complete & hand over

    We close out engineering, survey and environmental requirements so your project can be signed off and handed over with confidence.

    Final survey · Compliance · Handover
