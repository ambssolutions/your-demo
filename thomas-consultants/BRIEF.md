## Services (6)
1. Land Surveying: We provide accurate and reliable data for your development projects. (boundary redefinition, cross-lease to freehold, topographical, set out, drone inspections, 3D laser scanning)
2. RMA Planning: Advice on zoning, land use and resource consent applications. (feasibility reports, hearings evidence, plan changes)
3. Engineering: Infrastructure that is robust and compliant. (roads, stormwater, three waters, earthworks, flood engineering, construction monitoring)
4. Landscape Architecture: Every aspect of the outdoor design process. (open space design, landscape & visual assessments, planting plans)
5. Environmental Services: Environmental Management Plans, ecology, soil & water contamination testing, asbestos.
6. Project Management: From concept to completion, we oversee the entire process.

## Why choose us
After 25 years of delivering multi-faceted land development and infrastructure projects, our expertise is second to none... committed to environmentally responsible and sustainable development... transparent communication and seamless project management, timely results that are on budget and aligned with your vision.
